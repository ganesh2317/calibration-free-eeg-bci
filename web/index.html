💡 Sensorimotor Rhythm (ERD/ERS) Physiological Context & Domain Adaptation

Contra-lateral Desynchronization (ERD): Left-hand motor imagery typically attenuates μ (8–12 Hz) and β (16–24 Hz) oscillations over the right sensorimotor cortex (channel C4), while right-hand imagery attenuates oscillations over the left sensorimotor cortex (channel C3).

Inter-Subject Non-Stationarity: Across different human subjects, skull geometry, electrode impedance, and cortical folding vary dramatically, causing severe covariate shift in standard deep models.

Domain Invariance (DANN): The Domain-Adversarial Neural Network uses a Gradient Reversal Layer (GRL) during source subject training to encourage the feature extractor to discard subject-identifying noise while preserving motor intent.

Dataset Specifications

  • Cohort: 10 subjects (S001–S010)
  • Total Trials: 900 epochs
  • Per Subject: 90 epochs (45 Left, 45 Right)
  • Class Balance: Perfectly balanced (50.0% / 50.0%)

Signal Parameters

  • Sampling Rate: 160.0 Hz
  • Electrode Array: 64 EEG channels
  • Epoch Duration: 4.0 seconds (640 timepoints)
  • FIR Bandpass: 8.0 – 30.0 Hz zero-phase

Validation Protocol

  • Scheme: 10-Fold LOSO
  • Calibration: Exactly 0 calibration trials
  • Train / Val / Test Split: 8 source / 1 val / 1 held-out test
  • Leakage Prevention: Strict isolation
